# COCO Island Holidays - Modern Tailwind Conversion

## 🎉 Conversion Complete!

This project has been successfully converted from legacy Bootstrap/jQuery templates to modern **Tailwind CSS** with clean, responsive design.

## 📁 New Template Structure

### Modern Tailwind Templates (Active)
- `resources/views/modern-home.blade.php` - Homepage with hero video and tour listings
- `resources/views/modern-about.blade.php` - About page with company story and values
- `resources/views/modern-contact.blade.php` - Contact page with form and map
- `resources/views/modern-packages.blade.php` - Tour packages with dynamic content
- `resources/views/modern-tour-show.blade.php` - Individual tour detail pages
- `resources/views/modern-privacy-policy.blade.php` - Privacy policy page
- `resources/views/modern-terms-conditions.blade.php` - Terms and conditions page

### Legacy Templates (Archived)
All old Bootstrap-based templates have been moved to `resources/views/legacy-templates/` for reference.
